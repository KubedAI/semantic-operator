## How the three fit together

Authoring happens once, by a person. Reconciliation and serving are continuous and
independent. They coordinate only through the Kubernetes API, never by calling each other.

## Known limits

Engine credentials are a static username and password from a Secret. Per-caller identity is
available through passthrough or token exchange, but IAM, short-lived credentials, and
key-pair schemes are not built yet. The full list of operational limits, including engine
count, served namespaces, and artifact size, is in
[Configuration and deployment](/reference/configuration).
